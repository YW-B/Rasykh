# Rasykh – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: March 2026_

Rasykh is a Qur’an memorization app focused on offline Hifz (Mutasawi method). We take your privacy seriously and designed the app so that your data stays on your device.

## 1. Data we collect

We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal data that can identify you.

The app does **not** require:
- Account signup or login
- Email address or phone number
- Access to your contacts, photos, or files

## 2. Data stored on your device

The app stores some data **only on your device** to make the features work:

- Memorization progress (per surah, per chunk, repetitions, streaks)
- Last position (where you stopped reciting)
- App settings (theme, language, preferences)

This data is stored locally using the standard storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. It is not sent to our servers because we do not operate any backend for this app in the current version.

If you uninstall the app, this local data is removed from your device.

## 3. Audio and network usage

The app streams Qur’an audio from third‑party Quran audio CDNs in order to play full‑surah recitation.

- Only the **audio files** are requested from these providers.
- We do **not** send them any personal information from inside the app.
- Network providers or the audio CDN may see your IP address as part of normal internet traffic.

We use this audio only for non‑commercial, educational purposes.

## 4. Donations and payments

The app may show voluntary donation options (e.g., bank transfer, Instapay, or external links such as PayPal).

- All donations are processed **outside** the app by the payment provider or your bank.
- We do **not** collect or store your card details or banking information inside the app.

Please review the privacy policies of any external payment providers you use.

## 5. Analytics and tracking

The app currently:

- Does **not** use third‑party analytics SDKs.
- Does **not** use tracking for advertising.
- Does **not** show ads.

## 6. Children’s privacy

Rasykh is suitable for users of all ages and is intended for educational/religious use. Since we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we ever introduce cloud sync, accounts, or analytics in future versions, this privacy policy will be updated and clearly reflected in the app and on this page.
